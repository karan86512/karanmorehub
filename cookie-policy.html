What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information and provide a better experience.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • To improve website functionality.
  • To understand visitor behavior.
  • To remember user preferences.
  • To improve our services and content.

KaranMoreHub may use Google AdSense or other advertising partners to show relevant advertisements.

These third-party services may use cookies to display ads based on users' interests and previous browsing activity.

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use our website, which helps us improve performance and user experience.

Managing Cookies
